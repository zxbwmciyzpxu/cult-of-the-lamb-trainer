## 📖 About

Cult of the Lamb by Massive Monster (2022) blends roguelite dungeon runs with base-building cult management. You are a lamb chosen by a dark god, building a following of animals while crusading through procedural biomes. Balancing cult needs and dungeon runs becomes overwhelming mid-game.

| Problem | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Cult member faith depletes overnight requiring constant ritual scheduling | ✅ Solved |
| Food supply collapses during long crusade absences killing followers | ✅ Solved |
| Dungeon curse cards stack to unmanageable levels in later biomes | ✅ Solved |
| Tarot deck lacks critical combat cards when boss fights demand them | ✅ Solved |
| Follower illness spreads unchecked without frequent shrine upgrades | ✅ Solved |

The **Cult of the Lamb Trainer** solves all these issues, giving you complete control so you can focus on enjoying the game.
